A fast elemental card duel for [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/best-card-game-20261122-1826): you vs Cinder, the rival. Both start at 20 HP with a hand of 5 cards. Each round you both play one card — highest effective power wins the round and deals the difference as damage.

- **Flame beats Leaf · Leaf beats Wave · Wave beats Flame (+3)** — suits told by colour and shape, never text alone.
- **Heal +5** and **Ember Bolt (6 flat)** special cards.
- Cinder reads your last play and favours the element that beats it, saves her Heal for when she is hurt, and otherwise plays her strongest strike — so a low card can bait out her big one.

Play it: serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html`. Click a card (or press 1–5) to play it, Space to start again.
